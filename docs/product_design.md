## Product Roadmap User Stories

### Pre-Alpha Release

1. As a Subscriber, I would like to see my history of workouts, so that I can analyze my progress
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to select a previous workout from my history, so that I can quickly get to a workout in my rotation
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to discover a new workout from a Creator, so that I can mix up my routine and stay motivated
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to identify a workout and see its details, so that I can decide whether or not I want to do it
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to conduct a workout that has 3 sets of 10 squats
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to review analysis of my workout, so that I can gauge my progress
1. As a Subscriber, I would like to have my squat reps automatically counted, so that I don't have to log my own data

### Unsorted/Post-MVP

1. **[Account 1]** As a Prospective User, I would like to log in using my Facebook, Instagram, or Google accounts, so that I can easily use the app's features without a lengthy registration loop.
1. **[Workout 1]** As a Subscriber, I would like to conduct a workout that tracks and logs my reps for an exercise, so that I can seamlessly run through a set.
1. **[Workout 2]** As a User, I would like to start a workout quickly after opening the app. 
1. **[Log 1]** As a User, I would like to see my progress over the course of multiple workouts.
1. **[Workout 3]** As a User, I would like app-provided workouts, so that I don't have to create my own.
1. **[Workout 4]** As a User, I would like to modify app-provided workouts to suit my needs.

1. As a User, I would like to create and share my own workouts.
1. As a User, I would like to receive actionable feedback on proper form.
1. As a User, I would like motivational reminders to keep working out.
